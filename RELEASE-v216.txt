V216 — DASHBOARD VIEWPORT FILL + DEPARTMENT GRID FIT

Changes are layout-only on top of v215.

1. Dashboard analytics card
- Quality and shared-department dashboards now calculate the exact remaining desktop viewport height at runtime.
- The analytics card fills the remaining space below the four summary cards.
- The layout recalculates on dashboard changes and browser resize.
- Desktop dashboard stays no-scroll; mobile/tablet behavior is unchanged.

2. Department/Admin card screen
- Uses wider desktop workspace (up to 1440px).
- 5 columns on desktop and 6 columns on wider desktop screens.
- Department and Admin cards are more compact vertically.
- All department cards + the three locked/unlocked Admin cards fit within the desktop viewport without page scrolling.

No data, Firebase, login, notification, KPI, Time Sheet, backup, or role logic was changed.
