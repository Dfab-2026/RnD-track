QUALITY WORKSPACE — v129

Deploy with your existing Firebase config and publish the included firestore.rules.
Quality uses quality_projects, quality_entries and quality_records, separate from
R&D and Production. No existing data is moved or deleted.

Pages:
- Internal Audit: select any active department and Pending/In progress/Completed.
  Pending and In progress require remarks; Completed requires no issue remarks.
- Department-wise Quality KPI: department, KPI name and completion status.
- Training: candidate, role, training start/end and outcomes.
- Organisation Document Audit: today's automatic date and record-maintenance status.
- Incoming Inspection: In progress until Report submitted is checked and its
  report reference is entered. It then saves as Completed.
- Kaizen: one idea per person per day; weekly target 5, Monday–Sunday.
- Time Sheet: the same project assignment, clock selection, work/remarks,
  saved history and hourly reminder workflow, in Quality-only collections.

Enter your name on Quality pages. Each record includes its author and date.
Saved records are visible to the Quality workspace; editing requires entering
the record owner's name, following the existing name-based access model.
This is not verified user authentication. Browser preferences stay local.
Business records save to Firebase, with live updates and visible save errors.
Dashboard contents are left for the next requested specification.

Live Firebase verification needs your real configuration and deployed rules.
