QUALITY DAILY WORK — v135

Quality only: Dashboard, Daily Ideas and Daily Task are first in the sidebar.
Inspections, ORG document readiness and coordination are grouped into Daily Task.
All other sections, R&D and Production are preserved.

Daily Task has three full-width cards in order. A signed-in member submits one
record for each card per day. Completed hides remarks and gives a green 1/1.
Pending/In progress requires remarks and remains red 0/1 on Dashboard, even
after its submission is saved. Saving marks the form Submitted and moves focus
to the next card. Edit/Delete operate on that person's current-day submissions.
The Inspection card includes Incoming/Inprocess/Final stage selection.

Daily Ideas: Navarasu and Mohan each submit one idea per day, with problem,
proposal and expected benefit. Own entries support Edit/Delete. Team submissions
and pending names appear on Dashboard. Weekly/monthly Daily Ideas KPI filters
count the actual saved ideas: 1/day, 6/working week, Monday–Saturday monthly target.
Kaizen remains its separate original module and does not automatically duplicate
Daily Ideas. Existing old inspection/document/coordination history is preserved
and remains included in the Quality KPI Report's historical status summary.
